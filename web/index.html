DATA MODEL

About this prototype

Campus profile

Academic departments, hostels, food services, auditorium, sports and shared facilities.

Home profile

Living room, bedrooms, kitchen, work area, bathrooms, laundry and outdoor loads.

Calculation approach

Area energy is summed for daily and monthly estimates. Demand readings are compared across two halves of the sample period and classified using configurable power bands.

Data limitations

Inputs are simulated and editable. Equipment shares are estimated allocations, not sensor measurements or actual device telemetry.
